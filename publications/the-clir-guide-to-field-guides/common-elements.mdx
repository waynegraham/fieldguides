## Direct. Succinct. Navigable.

The following outline of the common elements of the Field Guide series indicates the purpose of each section. Authors should include or exclude elements as appropriate for their project, and they should customize the titles of each section to align with their content.

In addition to these elements, each Field Guide should include high–resolution visuals that underscore major points in the narrative, demonstrate statistical or other evidentiary basis for its findings, or illustrate how readers can put the Guide’s recommendations into practice. Links to external resources are allowed but should be kept to a minimum and primarily limited to the “References and Recommended Resources” section of the Guide.

## Field Guide Series Model Outline

Every Guide will not necessarily include every section listed below, or order them in the same sequence.

- Overview of the Guide
  - This section will help readers understand whether and how the Guide’s content will be relevant to them, helping them recommend it to others they know even if it’s not their own area of expertise.
- Why the Guide is important
  - This section is a deeper exploration of the topic’s significance in the current moment, including how a better knowledge or appreciation of the topic can help inform decisions and practices that will benefit research, learning, and the public good.
- The Guide’s topic in a broader context
  - This section outlines relevant historical, social, economic, technological, or political changes relevant to understanding the topic and its significance for memory organizations today.
- A breakdown of the topic’s relevance, dimensions, or implications for different types of organizations, roles, or use cases
  - This section details how an understanding of the topic can inform decisions, workflows, policies, and practices in memory organizations.
- Examples or cases that further illustrate the dimensions or implications of the topic
  - This section offers hypothetical or real-world examples of situations in which knowledge covered in the Guide can be applied.
- Step-by-step guidance for how to apply an understanding of the topic
  - This section outlines a plan of action that a reader can take to implement changes in their own organization using insights gleaned from the Guide. This section may include decision trees, worksheets, assessment instruments, or other resources that can be used to customize the Guide’s recommendations for their own needs.
- Summary of key principles and suggestions for further exploration
  - This section revisits and underlines major insights or principles presented within the Guide and suggests related topics deserving of more widespread attention and investigation.
- References and recommended resources
  - This section provides full bibliographic information for all sources cited in the Guide, and, separately, lists other related readings or resources readers can use to learn more.
- About the authors
  - This section provides 2-3 sentences of biodata for each named author. It may also include a summary of the authors’ other work together as a group.
