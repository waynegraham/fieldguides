## What is this?

This guide outlines the purpose, policies, and procedures for preparing a work for the CLIR Field Guides series. It is intended for use by CLIR staff and commissioned authors.

## Overview and Objectives

CLIR’s Field Guide series was created in 2026 in order to address emerging needs and concerns affecting the day-to-day work of librarians, archivists, and other cultural heritage workers around the world. The series prioritizes topics not addressed elsewhere and issues that stretch or transcend traditional concepts of stewardship. Works are designed both to orient readers to evolving issues impacting research and memory organizations and to facilitate new collaborations and actions that address those issues constructively. Both practical and speculative, CLIR Field Guides introduce concepts in ways that readers can grasp quickly while demonstrating how they can be put to use in order to make information resources more durable, coherent, and broadly useful for the creation and dissemination of knowledge.
