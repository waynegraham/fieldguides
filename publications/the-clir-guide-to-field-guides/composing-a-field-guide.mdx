## Collaborative. Quick. Changeable.

Each Guide will be commissioned by invitation prior to composition from a collaborating group of authors with relevant expertise. These authors commit to augment and revise the content of the guide for a period of one (1) year following its initial release, responding to community input. Content of the Guides should be compliant with the current CLIR Style Guide and with the current edition of the Chicago Manual of Style, using author-date parenthetical citations as needed. The specific audience(s) for each Guide should dictate its content, language, and design, and authors should anticipate how the audience(s) will access and read the content: by laptops, phones, tablets, other mobile devices, or in print.

Before beginning their manuscript, authors should consult with CLIR staff about the fitness of their ideas for the series and prepare a detailed proposal for the project that includes a rationale for publication, preliminary outline, and plan of work that includes a timeline.
